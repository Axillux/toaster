Last updated: August 2026

## The Service

Toaster ("the Bot") is an automated Discord moderation service. It detects scam content (such as MrBeast-style giveaway scam images and texts), removes it, applies the punishments configured by server administrators, and helps clean up past scam activity.

## Acceptance

By adding the Bot to a Discord server or using its commands, you accept these Terms on behalf of that server. If you do not accept them, remove the Bot.

## Eligibility

You must comply with Discord's Terms of Service, including the minimum age requirement. The Bot is provided for server moderation only.

## Automated moderation

- The Bot acts automatically: it may delete messages, timeout, kick or ban users, and send direct messages to punished users.
- Detection is probabilistic. **False positives and false negatives can happen.** Administrators are responsible for reviewing incidents (log channel, review cards) and for choosing punishments that fit their community.
- Consequences of moderation actions — including mistaken ones — rest with the server administration, not the Bot operator.

## Acceptable use

- Do not try to evade, probe or overload the detection system, including by flooding the Bot.
- Do not use the Bot to harass users or to moderate content it was not built for.
- Do not misrepresent the Bot's actions to your members.

## Availability
