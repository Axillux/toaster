Toaster is a bot that deletes MrBeast, Andrew Tate and etc. scams. (crypto casinos to be short). And yeah that's it.

What it does

  • Real-time detection. AI compares every message's text and images against a scam database.
  • Instant action. The scam is deleted, the scammer gets a DM, then the punishment you pick: timeout, kick, or ban.
  • Whole-wave cleanup. Every message the scammer sent is removed from all channels, re-checked until clean.
  • Cross-server. One detection removes the account from every server Toaster is in.
  • History backfill. /scan cleans up to 90 days of old scam. Dry-run mode previews first.

Commands

  • /scan days:90 scans up to 90 days of history, deletes found scam and punishes senders. Add dry_run:True to preview without deleting.
  • /settings configures punishment, mute duration, log channel and the DM template.
  • /report shows detection stats for this server or globally.
  • /about shows what the bot is and how many servers it protects.
  • /avatar shows a user's avatar in full size.
  • /legal posts the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service links.
  • /ping checks latency.
